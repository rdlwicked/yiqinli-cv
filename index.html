UCL Research Assistant · April – October 2026

RAI: Recursive Accelerated Imitation for Robot Manipulation

Interactive Imitation Learning · Contact-Rich Manipulation

RAI closes the loop between imitation learning and deployment: each round executes the current policy faster, records its rollouts with targeted human corrections, and trains the next policy on the accumulated experience. The multimodal flow-matching policy combines vision, proprioception, and estimated contact forces.

During my research assistantship at UCL, my work on the project spanned real-robot teleoperation, data collection and conversion, and real-time chunked policy deployment, connecting 30 Hz policy actions to 200 Hz arm control.

Dexterous-Hand VLA Pipeline

Real Robot Pipeline · Teleoperation · 22-DoF Arm-Hand Coordination

At Shanghai Jiao Tong University, I built the teleoperation, data collection, and evaluation stack for a LinkerHand O6 + xArm6 platform. This work grounds the real-robot side of my current interests, especially contact-rich data collection, dexterous action spaces, and deployment-time evaluation.

Language-Guided Imitation Learning

MEng Thesis · Language-Conditioned Manipulation · Closed-Loop Recovery

My MEng thesis built a closed-loop system where an LLM planner decomposes natural-language commands into a sub-task DAG and a behaviour-tree controller orchestrates perception, policy execution, and automatic recovery. The project achieved 100% success on pick-and-place and a 2.4× improvement over a code-as-policy baseline.

Thesis

Evo-SOTA — VLA Benchmark Leaderboard

Benchmarking Infrastructure · VLA Landscape Analysis · Open Source

I built and maintain an open-source leaderboard comparing Vision-Language-Action models across eight benchmarks, including LIBERO, CALVIN, Meta-World, RoboChallenge, and RoboTwin 2.0, alongside a dexterous-manipulation track. This project helps me systematically compare architectures, training data sources, and post-training strategies across the current VLA landscape.
